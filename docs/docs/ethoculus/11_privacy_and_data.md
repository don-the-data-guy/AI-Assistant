# Privacy and Data Handling

Ethoculus is designed to be local-first, but local-first does not automatically mean risk-free.

Users should understand what they are running, what they upload, and where their data may be stored.

---

## Local-First Does Not Mean Harmless

A local AI system can still create risk if users upload sensitive information, expose the service to a network, install unsafe extensions, or misunderstand where files are stored.

Before using Ethoculus with sensitive material, ask:

- Where is the app running?
- Who can access the machine?
- Are Docker volumes being backed up?
- Are uploaded files stored locally?
- Is the app exposed beyond localhost?
- Are third-party models or APIs being used?

---

## Sensitive Data Warning

Avoid uploading sensitive data unless you understand the risks.

Sensitive data may include:

- Legal documents
- Medical records
- Financial records
- Passwords or secrets
- Private client information
- Personal identifying information
- Immigration documents
- Employment records
- Insurance claim materials

---

## Professional Use Warning

If you use Ethoculus for professional work, you remain responsible for confidentiality, privilege, compliance, licensing, and human review.

AI output may be wrong.

AI output may sound confident and still be unsupported.

---

## Recommended Practice

For important work:

1. Use the smallest necessary document set.
2. Remove unnecessary personal information.
3. Ask the model to cite the source.
4. Read the cited source yourself.
5. Treat the AI answer as a draft, not a final decision.
6. Keep human judgment central.

---

## Network Exposure Warning

The starter version is intended for local use.

Do not expose Ethoculus to the public internet unless you understand authentication, access control, HTTPS, backups, logging, and security hardening.
